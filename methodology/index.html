Methodology & sources

How SimpleKit turns assumptions into planning estimates.

SimpleKit calculators are educational planning tools. The aim is to make the important math visible, keep assumptions understandable, and use primary Canadian sources when a result depends on government or regulatory rules.

Calculation philosophy

Useful first answer, transparent path to the details.

Formulas and assumptions

Each calculator should explain the assumptions that materially change its result. Defaults are starting points, not predictions. Where practical, users can replace them with values that better reflect their situation.

Estimates, not determinations

SimpleKit does not calculate an official tax assessment, government benefit entitlement, mortgage approval, or professional recommendation. Actual outcomes can depend on facts the calculator does not collect.

Rule-based values

When a tool depends on tax, pension, registered-account, mortgage, or similar rules, SimpleKit prefers the responsible Canadian government department, agency, Crown corporation, or regulator as the factual source.

Maintenance

Rule-based values are reviewed when tools are updated and when known rules change. Because rules can change between reviews, the primary source remains the authority for a current regulated value.

Primary Canadian references

Sources used to verify regulated assumptions.

These are reference authorities, not endorsements of SimpleKit. Individual calculator pages may link to the specific source relevant to their calculation.

CPP and public pensions

Mortgage affordability and insurance

CMHC publishes home-buying and debt-service guidance, while federal mortgage-insurance rules are published through Canada.ca.

Mortgage qualifying rate

OSFI publishes the minimum qualifying rate used for uninsured mortgages at federally regulated lenders.

OSFI — Minimum qualifying rate

See a value that may be outdated?

Rules change. If a calculator appears inconsistent with a current primary source, report it with the calculator name, the source, and enough detail to reproduce the issue.
