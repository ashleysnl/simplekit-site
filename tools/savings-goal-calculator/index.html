What it helps you answer

How long it could take to reach a savings target, how much you may need to save each month for a target date, and how interest changes the path along the way.

Why people use it

It gives you a calmer first-pass plan for a savings goal, then lets you compare multiple scenarios when you want to test different timelines, contribution levels, or assumptions.
