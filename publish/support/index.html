Support

Help keep the tools free, simple, and improving.

SimpleKit is designed to be useful without adding friction. Buy Me a Coffee support helps fund maintenance, UX improvements, new guides, and new planning tools while keeping the experience lightweight.

What support helps cover

Ongoing tool improvements, content expansion, design polish, hosting, and general platform maintenance.

Why it is optional

The goal is still simple access first. Support is there for people who found the tools helpful and want to help keep them moving forward.

If SimpleKit helped you move from uncertainty to a plan, this is the easiest way to support it.
