## Observed coverage and limits

Local Chromium captured 155 layouts: 54 substantive phone pages, 20 representatives across four widths, 12 populated calculator states, eight dark/forced-colour states, and the open homepage menu. The 13 compatibility pages have preserved canonical/noindex/handoff contracts and their canonical calculator destinations are photographed. Full-page captures include footers and long tables/charts; the upstream error route is explicitly included. No page errors or failed same-origin responses occurred locally. Existing overflow is reported instead of making the inventory fail as if the pages were already migrated.

Computed headings show the V2 homepage's locally bundled SimpleKit Display while editorial/calculator pages use Iowan Old Style/Baskerville/Georgia fallbacks. Site-owned legacy CSS and each source-owned stylesheet are traced in the inventory. Shared Core has 22 consumers; Core tokens/header/footer are shared, while calculators own their form/results/chart/table styling and implementation. Renaming typography alone will not provide coherent interiors.

The inventory is not WCAG certification, a content rewrite or approval of the whole-site design. Manual VoiceOver/Safari, NVDA/Firefox, devices/native zoom/contrast and field performance data remain separately pending. Migration checklist rows remain incomplete until their route-specific visual/function/SEO checks pass.
