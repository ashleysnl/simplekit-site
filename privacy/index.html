Privacy

SimpleKit keeps calculator use lightweight and account-free.

SimpleKit does not require an account to use its public calculators. Many calculations are performed by code running in your browser. The website also uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage and improve the tools.

Calculator inputs

The public calculators are designed to work without sending your financial inputs to a SimpleKit account or user database. Some tools may use browser storage so information can persist on your own device. Clearing browser data can remove locally stored information.

Analytics

SimpleKit loads Google Analytics to measure site usage such as page visits and interactions. Google may process analytics data according to its own terms and privacy practices. Calculator inputs are not intentionally configured as analytics event parameters.

What not to enter

Do not enter account numbers, passwords, social insurance numbers, tax identifiers, or other information that is unnecessary for a planning estimate. Use approximate planning values when exact personal data is not needed.

External links

SimpleKit links to government sources, GitHub, and other external services. Those destinations have their own privacy practices and are outside SimpleKit's control.

Questions or corrections?

If you notice a privacy concern or believe a tool is handling information differently from this description, report it without including private financial information.
