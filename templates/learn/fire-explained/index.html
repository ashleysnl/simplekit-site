FIRE Explained

FIRE stands for financial independence, retire early. In practical terms, it means building enough assets to support spending without relying on regular work income.

What matters most

Your spending level, savings rate, and timeline matter more than the label. Small changes in those inputs can change the path a lot.

Why context matters

A FIRE estimate is useful, but it should usually be compared against your current net worth and a fuller retirement plan.

What to watch for

Aggressive assumptions can make a plan look easier than it is. Testing different scenarios is part of using the concept well.

Use the right tools together

The FIRE Calculator, Net Worth Calculator, and Retirement Planner work best as a sequence rather than isolated estimates.
