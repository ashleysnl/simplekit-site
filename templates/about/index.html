About SimpleKit

SimpleKit is built for people who want a clearer plan without a complicated product.

SimpleKit is an independent project built and maintained by Ashley Skinner in Canada. The goal is to make common financial-planning questions easier to explore with transparent assumptions, plain-language explanations, and no account requirement.

What SimpleKit is

A connected suite of browser-based planners and calculators designed to turn a vague question into a workable plan.

Who it is for

People who want better planning tools without a login wall, a corporate dashboard, or unnecessary setup.

How it helps

Each tool focuses on a specific decision, and the platform connects those decisions through guides, related tools, and practical next steps.

What it is not

Not financial, legal, or tax advice. The tools are meant to support thinking and planning, not replace professional advice when stakes are high.

Who maintains SimpleKit

Built and maintained by Ashley Skinner.

SimpleKit is an independent Canadian project, not a bank, government service, accounting firm, or financial-advice practice. The tools are built for education and planning, and they do not make official eligibility, tax, lending, or benefit determinations.

Calculation approach

Start with a small number of useful inputs, show the assumptions that materially affect the result, and explain the result in plain language. Where a calculator depends on government or regulatory rules, SimpleKit prefers primary Canadian sources.

Read the methodology and source policy

Maintenance approach

Rule-based values are reviewed when tools are updated and when known Canadian rules change. A calculator may still lag a newly announced rule, so important decisions should be checked against the linked primary source or an appropriate professional.

Corrections and questions

Found an outdated assumption, unclear explanation, or calculation that looks wrong? Report the tool, input, expected result, and what you observed so it can be investigated.

Report a correction or contact SimpleKit

Privacy approach

SimpleKit does not require an account. Many calculations run in your browser, but the site also uses Google Analytics for site-usage measurement. Do not enter information you would not want processed in a browser-based tool.

Read the privacy information
