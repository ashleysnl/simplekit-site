RRSP vs TFSA Basics

The core difference is timing. RRSP contributions can create a deduction now, while TFSA withdrawals can be tax-free later. Which one fits better depends on your broader situation.

RRSP basics

RRSPs can be especially useful when a deduction today matters and when future withdrawals fit your longer-term tax picture.

TFSA basics

TFSAs offer flexibility and tax-free withdrawals, which can be useful when you want easier access or different tax treatment later.

Why there is no universal winner

Income level, retirement plans, time horizon, and flexibility needs all affect which account may be more useful.
