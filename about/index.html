About SimpleKit

SimpleKit is built for people who want a clearer plan without a complicated product.

The goal is simple: help people model important decisions faster, with less friction, less jargon, and clearer next steps from the start.

What SimpleKit is

A connected suite of browser-based planners and calculators designed to turn a vague question into a workable plan.

Who it is for

People who want better planning tools without a login wall, a corporate dashboard, or unnecessary setup.

How it helps

Each tool focuses on a specific decision, and the platform connects those decisions through guides, related tools, and practical next steps.

What it is not

Not financial, legal, or tax advice. The tools are meant to support thinking and planning, not replace professional advice when stakes are high.

Start simple. Go deeper only when you need to.

That is the core idea behind the platform, whether you are planning retirement income, comparing account strategies, or deciding how much room you have for a trip.
